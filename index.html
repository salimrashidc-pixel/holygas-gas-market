Methodology
How every number on this page is computed
Headroom = Total supply (Gestor, all processes) − Thermal gas burn (XM)
Supply. Gestor del Mercado daily energy per field (MBTU/day), all production types and processes. National-only supply is compared against the declared production potential (PP) from the DPNG resolution in force; imported gas is excluded from that ratio. Process categories are shown as published; their exact overlap should be validated with the Gestor before summing across all five.

Thermal burn. XM ConsCombustibleMBTU per resource, keeping fuel names containing GAS (domestic and imported). Implied heat rate = MBTU burned ÷ MWh generated (Gene per resource); the 30-day median is used as the plant reference, bounded 4–20 MBTU/MWh. Dual-fuel plants dilute the ratio.

Maturity. XM settles demand and fuel data over several days; values younger than five days are labelled preliminary and are re-pulled on every refresh until final. Balance figures use the latest date on which both supply and burn are mature.

Units. GBTUD for national aggregates, MBTUD for fields and plants, GWh-day for power, MBTU/MWh for heat rates. Transporter volumes in their native units (KPC) with the PC factor from settings.
